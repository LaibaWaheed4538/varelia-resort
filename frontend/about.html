Handcrafted Architecture & Timeless Seclusion

Nestled beneath the towering sentinel peaks of Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar, VARELIA was conceived as a sanctuary that honors the sacred geography of Gilgit-Baltistan.

Every chalet is built using time-honored dry-stone masonry and intricate Swat walnut woodwork, blending effortlessly into the rugged cliffs while offering ultra-luxurious heated interiors, panoramic observatory glass, and private butler service.

Guests can step directly from their private terraces onto ancient Silk Road trails, accompanied by veteran mountain masters and cultural historians.
